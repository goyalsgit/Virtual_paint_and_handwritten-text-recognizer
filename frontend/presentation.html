The Need for Touchless Systems Devansh Goyal

🏥 Sterile Environments

In surgical operating suites and pharmaceutical cleanrooms, keyboards and mice are primary vectors for cross-contamination. Physical touch requires tedious de-gloving cycles.

♿ Accessibility Gap

Traditional mice and keyboards require fine-motor grip. Voice interfaces leak sensitive document data and fail completely in noisy clinical or industrial settings.

✋ Natural Air-Writing

Hand gestures and air-drawing represent the most intuitive, high-degree-of-freedom human interface—requiring zero wearable sensors or expensive depth cameras.

Our Vision: Deliver an accessible, 100% browser-based, touchless document interaction platform powered purely by an ordinary webcam.

Dataset Acquisition & Stroke Preprocessing Divyansh Chhabra

The Domain Gap

  • Pretrained TrOCR: Exclusively trained on IAM paper handwriting.
  • Air-Writing Reality: Lacks ink bleeding, has thinner digital strokes, variable writing scale, and hand tremors.
  • Our Solution: Engineered an in-app dataset collector acquiring 400+ custom air-drawn samples across multiple participants.

Mathematical Preprocessing Pipeline

  • 1. Ink Mask & Bounding Crop: Isolates active strokes with 24px protective padding.
  • 2. Aspect-Ratio Square Centering: Zero-pads to 384×384 to preserve character proportions.
  • 3. Morphological Elliptical Dilation: Applies cv2.dilate with (5×5) structuring kernel to thicken digital air strokes, aligning them with paper handwriting distributions.

Module 3: Gesture Media Player Keshav Laddha

Unified Touchless Playback

  • Supports both Local Video Files and Embedded YouTube Streams.
  • OK Sign: Instant Play / Pause toggle.
  • Thumbs Up / Down: Fluid volume adjustment.
  • Horizontal Swipe: 10-second forward / backward seek.

Temporal Hysteresis Stability

  • Natural hand motion frequently produces transitory intermediate finger states.
  • AirDoc employs a Finite State Machine with 4-frame confirmation: a gesture must remain stable for ≥130ms before executing an action.
  • Cooldown guards prevent repeated unintended trigger loops.

✨ New: Two-Tier Spell Correction Engine Shivam Gupta

Tier 1: Fast Local SymSpell

Implemented in backend/spell_correction.py

  • Uses Levenshtein edit distance ($\text{cutoff} \le 2$) with frequency ranking.
  • Incorporates domain-specific vocabulary extracted from dataset labels and custom wordlists.
  • Executes in <5ms with zero network dependency.

Tier 2: Contextual LLM Assistance

Implemented in backend/llm_spell_correction.py

  • Powered by gpt-4o-mini running in non-blocking async threads.
  • Resolves OCR split tokens (e.g., handles "I S" → "is" and "arm" → "am" based on full phrase context).
  • Presents up to 5 ranked candidates selectable via touchless gestures.

✨ New: Shape Assist & Virtual Controls Shivam Gupta

Intelligent Shape Assist (shape-assist.js)

  • Douglas-Peucker Simplification: Filters noisy trajectory data.
  • Circle & Ellipse Fitting: Computes contour circularity metric ($\frac{4\pi \cdot \text{Area}}{\text{Perimeter}^2} \approx 1$).
  • Polygon & Box Fitting: Detects corner orthogonality ($90^\circ \pm \epsilon$).
  • Straight Lines & Arrows: Automatically snaps freehand arrows and vectors into crisp presentation diagrams.

Touchless Virtual Floating Buttons

  • Zero Peripheral Interaction: On-canvas floating buttons for 9 colors, eraser, undo, and clear.
  • 500ms Dwell-Time Selection: Pointing at a button fills a circular radial progress ring.
  • Provides tactile visual feedback and prevents accidental touch triggers.
